# Repeat Guest Engine — Founder Delivery Playbook

## Commercial scope

Founder Launch: €690 one-time for one property, limited to the first 3 completed purchases through the dedicated Stripe checkout.

Optional managed service: €129/month only if separately agreed. Do not imply that it is included in the €690 launch.

## What the client receives

1. Setup review of the property's current PMS/export structure, direct-booking path, languages and marketing rules.
2. Mapping of one clean guest CSV/PMS export into the agreed fields.
3. Separation of marketing-eligible contacts from suppressed/unsubscribed records according to information supplied/confirmed by the property.
4. Practical segmentation, normally including recent, dormant/win-back, low-season and value/language cohorts where the available data supports them.
5. Three core workflow configurations: post-stay, win-back and low-season reactivation.
6. Test and approval stage before promotional launch.
7. Basic reporting/operating view.
8. Staff handover and ownership notes.

## Explicit exclusions unless separately quoted

- Manual cleaning of severely malformed databases.
- Custom PMS/API integrations that are not available in the standard implementation path.
- Copywriting/translations beyond the agreed launch messages.
- Paid media spend.
- Third-party software, hosting, messaging, email/API, payment or channel fees.
- Legal advice or determination of the property's lawful basis for marketing.
- Guaranteed revenue, bookings, occupancy or conversion rates.
- Multi-property rollout.

## Sales call — 15 minute structure

### Minute 0–3: diagnose
Ask:
- Which PMS / booking system do you use?
- Roughly how many past guest records do you have?
- Do you currently send post-stay or win-back campaigns?
- Which periods are hardest to fill?
- What direct booking destination should a returning guest use?

### Minute 3–8: show the demo
Use `/repeat-guest-engine/demo`.
Explain the four steps: Import → Segment → Automate → Approve & launch.
State clearly that all numbers and contacts in the demo are synthetic.

### Minute 8–12: define fit
Good fit if the property has usable historical guest data, a direct-booking destination, and wants a repeatable retention workflow.
Poor fit if there is effectively no usable guest history, no way to accept a direct booking, or the property cannot confirm which contacts may receive promotional communications.

### Minute 12–15: close
If fit is clear, explain the Founder Launch: €690 for the first three completed purchases. Do not send the checkout before the buyer understands the scope. If they are ready, send the dedicated Founder Stripe checkout.

## Onboarding after payment

Do not ask for passwords or a live database over ordinary email as the first step.

Collect:
- Property legal/trading name and main operational contact.
- Website and direct-booking URL.
- PMS / source of the export.
- Approximate database size.
- Available fields in the export.
- Languages required.
- Target low-season periods.
- Existing consent / suppression / unsubscribe information.
- Brand voice and offer approval contact.

Before live data transfer, agree the secure transfer method and clarify data responsibilities.

## Delivery sequence

Day 0–1: kickoff and export-field review.
Day 1–3: mapping, suppression logic and segments.
Day 3–5: configure the three core workflows and draft property-facing messages.
Day 5–7: testing, property approval, reporting view and handover.

This is an internal target, not a public guaranteed SLA. Adjust if data quality or third-party dependencies require it.

## Founder case-study request

After the implementation has operated long enough to generate meaningful data, ask the property for permission to use aggregated, non-identifying results as a case study. Never publish the property name, guest information or performance data without permission.

Track at minimum:
- eligible audience size,
- delivered sends,
- opens/clicks where supported,
- direct-booking actions attributable to the campaign where technically measurable,
- campaign period,
- offer used.

Do not claim causal revenue if attribution is uncertain.

## Internal economics to record per client

Track actual hours spent in:
- sales/call,
- data mapping,
- workflow configuration,
- copy/translation,
- testing,
- handover,
- post-launch support.

Also record third-party variable costs. After the first three Founder implementations, calculate contribution margin before deciding the permanent launch price and commission structure.
