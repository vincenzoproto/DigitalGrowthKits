# GuestFlow Systems — Repeat Guest Engine Partner Kit

## What partners are introducing
Repeat Guest Engine helps an independent hotel turn usable past-guest history into a structured repeat-booking workflow through segmentation plus three core automations: post-stay, win-back and low-season reactivation.

This is not positioned as a replacement for the hotel's PMS, booking engine, revenue manager or agency. It should only be proposed where there is a genuine retention/CRM operating gap.

## Best partner profiles
- Revenue managers / revenue consultancies.
- Hospitality web and booking-engine agencies.
- PMS / hotel-tech consultants.
- Hotel sales and marketing consultants.
- Independent hospitality advisors with active hotel relationships.

## What the partner does
Minimum referral path:
1. Identify a property that may have usable historical guest data and a repeat-booking gap.
2. Make a warm introduction or provide the hotel contact with permission to approach.
3. GuestFlow runs the qualification/demo call.
4. GuestFlow scopes, implements and supports the standard delivery.

The partner does not need to deliver the technical setup unless a coordinated implementation is separately agreed.

## Partner pitch to an hotel
"You already have a historical guest database, but if it is not being used systematically for post-stay, win-back and low-season reactivation, I can introduce you to GuestFlow Systems. They first review the current setup; if your PMS already covers the need, there is no reason to duplicate it."

## Qualification checklist for referrals
Before introducing, try to confirm:
- independent property or small group,
- PMS / export available,
- meaningful past guest database,
- direct booking URL/path exists,
- interest in repeat bookings or weaker periods,
- no obvious complete retention system already active.

Do not ask partners to collect or transmit guest-level data.

## 15-minute partner call agenda
1. What kinds of properties do you currently support?
2. How many might have a repeat-guest/CRM gap?
3. Do you already provide CRM/email retention services?
4. Would referral-only or coordinated delivery be preferable?
5. How should attribution be recorded for a referral?
6. Pick one warm-fit hotel as the pilot introduction.

## Guardrails
- Do not promise guaranteed revenue, occupancy or bookings.
- Do not present scenario calculations as forecasts.
- Do not claim GDPR/legal compliance on behalf of the hotel.
- Do not collect passwords or guest databases during the sales stage.
- Do not position GuestFlow as replacing a working PMS/CRM stack unnecessarily.

## First-partner success metric
The goal is not "signing partners". The goal is one qualified warm introduction that reaches a scope-confirmed sales call.
