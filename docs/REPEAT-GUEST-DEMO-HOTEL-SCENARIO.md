# Repeat Guest Engine — Synthetic Demo Hotel Scenario

Use this only as a fictional sales walkthrough. Never present these numbers as a real customer case study.

## Property
**Hotel Aurora Firenze** — fictional independent 42-room boutique hotel.

Commercial situation:
- Strong April–October demand.
- Softer November, January and February.
- Direct booking page already active.
- Historical guest export available from PMS.
- No structured win-back programme.

## Synthetic database
2,000 historical guest rows.

Illustrative segmentation:
- 1,520 marketing-eligible contacts.
- 480 suppressed / ineligible / unsubscribed contacts.
- 410 recent guests (0–90 days).
- 620 dormant guests (180+ days).
- 360 previous low-season guests.
- 185 high-value historical stays (€500+ threshold).

Segments may overlap; counts must not be added together as unique total recipients.

## Workflow 1 — Post-stay
Audience: recent eligible guests.
Goal: maintain the direct relationship after checkout and provide a clear route for a future direct booking.

Example structure:
- delay after checkout,
- relationship / preference message,
- direct booking path,
- suppression/unsubscribe respected.

## Workflow 2 — Win-back
Audience: dormant eligible guests.
Goal: re-engage guests who have not stayed for 180+ days.

Example structure:
- language branch if supported by data,
- return reason / approved offer,
- direct booking destination,
- measurement tags where technically possible.

## Workflow 3 — Low season
Audience: eligible guests who historically travelled in low-season periods or match an approved segment.
Goal: fill weaker periods with a more relevant audience instead of a database-wide blast.

## Economic scenario — illustrative only
Assume for the walkthrough only:
- eligible audience used for a campaign: 1,000 contacts,
- historical average booking value: €420,
- hypothetical rebooking scenarios: 1%, 3%, 5%.

Scenario math:
- 1% = 10 bookings × €420 = €4,200 gross booking value.
- 3% = 30 bookings × €420 = €12,600 gross booking value.
- 5% = 50 bookings × €420 = €21,000 gross booking value.

These figures are arithmetic scenarios, **not forecasts, guarantees, expected performance, incremental profit or proven attribution**. Real conversion can be lower, zero or higher and depends on eligibility, offer, timing, demand, deliverability and many other factors.

## How to use this in a sales call
1. State immediately that Hotel Aurora is fictional.
2. Show the database segmentation.
3. Show why suppressed contacts never enter promotional audiences.
4. Show the three workflow previews.
5. Use the scenario calculator only to explain economic scale, not to predict results.
6. Ask the prospect: "Which of these three flows do you already have running today?"
7. If they answer none/partial, move to their actual PMS/export structure.

## Closing transition
"This is the fictional walkthrough. The useful question is whether your database can support the same operating structure. We can establish that in the setup review without receiving guest-level data on the first call."
