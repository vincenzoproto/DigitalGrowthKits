1. How many independent properties do you actively support?
2. Do those properties typically retain usable past-guest data in a PMS/export?
3. Do you already manage CRM/email retention for them?
4. Would you prefer referral-only or a coordinated implementation?
5. What would make this complementary rather than competitive with your current service?
6. Is there one warm-fit hotel you could introduce as the pilot?

Do not ask the partner to collect guest-level data. The first objective is one qualified warm introduction, not a large partner network on paper.
