# Repeat Guest Engine — Sales Kit

## Positioning
Do not sell this as "marketing automation software". Sell the business outcome:

**Turn past guest history into a structured repeat-booking channel.**

Core line: "You already paid to acquire these guests once. We help you build a controlled way to bring eligible past guests back to your direct booking path."

## Best-fit customer
- Independent hotel, boutique hotel or structured B&B.
- Usable PMS/CSV guest history.
- Clear direct-booking destination.
- At least one weak period, repeat-booking objective or retention gap.
- Someone on the property can confirm marketing eligibility, suppression and approvals.

Poor fit:
- No useful guest history.
- PMS already handles the full required workflow well.
- No direct-booking path.
- No reliable way to distinguish eligible vs suppressed contacts.
- Buyer expects guaranteed bookings.

## 15-minute call script

### 0–2 min — Frame
"I want to understand whether there is actually a gap worth solving. If your current PMS already does this properly, I would rather tell you not to buy another system."

### 2–5 min — Diagnose
Ask:
1. Which PMS / booking engine do you use?
2. Roughly how large is your historical guest database?
3. Do you currently run post-stay, win-back or low-season campaigns?
4. Which months or periods are hardest to fill?
5. Where does a returning guest book directly today?
6. Do you retain marketing eligibility / consent / suppression data?

### 5–9 min — Demo
Open `/repeat-guest-engine/demo`.
Show:
- CSV import processed locally in the browser.
- Eligible vs suppressed contacts.
- Recent, dormant, low-season and high-value segments.
- Three campaign previews.
- Scenario economics as assumptions only, not forecasts.

Explain the workflow: Import → Segment → Configure → Approve → Launch → Measure.

### 9–12 min — Confirm scope
Founder Launch covers one property:
- one clean PMS/CSV export mapping,
- segmentation,
- post-stay automation,
- win-back automation,
- low-season automation,
- basic reporting view,
- testing and approval,
- staff handover.

Explicitly separate third-party fees, heavy data cleanup, custom integrations and legal advice.

### 12–15 min — Close
If fit is clear:
"Your setup looks compatible with the standard Founder scope. The first three implementations are €690 one-time, then the standard setup is €990. If you want, the next step is to confirm the export structure and then reserve the Founder slot."

Do not send checkout before scope is understood.

## Qualification score
Score 1 point each:
- Database >500 usable records.
- Direct booking path exists.
- Weak/low-season period identified.
- No robust repeat-guest workflow already active.
- Marketing eligibility/suppression information available.
- Decision maker involved.

5–6 = strong fit.
3–4 = investigate.
0–2 = do not push sale.

## Fast objection answers

**"We already use Mailchimp/Brevo."**
Good. GuestFlow is not selling a newsletter tool; the value is hospitality-specific mapping, segmentation, workflow design, testing and handover. Existing sending infrastructure may be reused if suitable.

**"Our PMS sends emails already."**
Then the review checks whether it already covers the required reactivation logic. If it does, we should not duplicate it.

**"GDPR?"**
No promotional flow goes live until the property confirms the relevant eligibility/lawful basis and suppression information. GuestFlow does not provide legal advice.

**"Can you guarantee bookings?"**
No. We configure and measure the system; bookings depend on demand, offer, eligibility, timing and the property.

**"€690 is too much."**
This is a configured implementation for one property, not a prompt/template. If the operating value does not justify the setup price, the correct answer is not to force the sale.

**"Our database is messy."**
We can assess it. Heavy manual cleanup is outside the standard Founder scope and is quoted separately.

## Follow-up after a positive reply
1. Keep the same sender/thread.
2. Ask PMS, approximate DB size, current repeat-guest activity and direct-booking URL.
3. Book 15-minute review.
4. Update CRM immediately.
5. Only after fit is confirmed, send payment link.
