# Repeat Guest Engine - 15-minute sales call + objection playbook

## Call objective
Qualify fit, show the synthetic demo, define the launch scope, and only then offer the Founder Launch checkout if the property is ready.

## 15-minute structure

### 0-3 min - Diagnose
Ask:
- Which PMS or booking system do you use?
- Roughly how many past guest records do you have?
- Do you already run post-stay, win-back or low-season campaigns?
- Which months or periods are hardest to fill?
- Where should a returning guest book directly today?

### 3-7 min - Show the process
Open `/repeat-guest-engine/demo` and explain:
1. Import a clean PMS/CSV export.
2. Separate marketing-eligible and suppressed contacts.
3. Build practical segments.
4. Configure post-stay, win-back and low-season flows.
5. Test and approve before launch.

State clearly that the demo uses synthetic data and does not represent guaranteed performance.

### 7-11 min - Confirm fit
A good fit usually means:
- usable historical guest data,
- a direct-booking destination,
- at least one weak period or retention objective,
- a property contact who can approve messaging and confirm marketing eligibility.

### 11-15 min - Close
If fit is clear:
- Founder Launch: EUR 690 one-time, limited to the first 3 completed purchases.
- Standard launch price: EUR 990.
- Optional managed support: EUR 129/month, separate from launch.
- Send checkout only after scope is understood.

## Objection handling

### "We already have Mailchimp / Brevo / another email tool."
Response: Good - GuestFlow is not selling another newsletter tool. The work is the data mapping, eligibility logic, segmentation, three hospitality-specific flows, testing and handover. If the current sender is suitable, we can work around it rather than replacing it.

### "Our PMS already sends emails."
Response: That may cover transactional or basic automated messages. The review checks whether the current PMS can already do the required segmentation and reactivation. If it can, GuestFlow should not duplicate functionality; the project only makes sense where there is an operating gap.

### "We do not know whether we can contact old guests."
Response: Then we do not launch promotional sends yet. The first step is identifying what consent, suppression and unsubscribe information exists. The property remains responsible for confirming its lawful basis. GuestFlow does not provide legal advice.

### "EUR 690 is too much."
Response: The Founder price is for a configured one-property implementation, not a template download. It includes mapping, segmentation, three workflows, reporting view and handover. If that scope is not valuable enough for the property, it is better not to force the project.

### "Can you guarantee bookings?"
Response: No. We can configure and measure a repeat-booking system, but demand, offer quality, guest eligibility and attribution vary by property. We do not guarantee revenue, occupancy or conversion rates.

### "Can you just send a campaign for us?"
Response: We can discuss a smaller custom scope, but the standard Founder Launch is designed to leave the property with a repeatable system rather than a one-off blast.

### "We have a very messy database."
Response: The review can assess structure, but heavy manual cleanup is not included in the standard Founder Launch and may need a separate quote.

### "Can you work with multiple hotels?"
Response: The Founder price is one property. Multi-property rollout is scoped separately after the first implementation is validated.

## Closing line
"If the operating gap is real, the next step is simple: we confirm the export structure and direct-booking path, then start the Founder implementation. If the gap is not real, I would rather tell you not to buy it."
