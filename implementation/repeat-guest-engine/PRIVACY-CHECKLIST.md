# Repeat Guest Engine — Data & Privacy Checklist

Before importing a customer database:

1. Property confirms it controls the guest data and is authorized to provide it for the agreed processing purpose.
2. Property confirms which contacts are eligible for promotional marketing and provides the source/status used to determine this.
3. Remove unnecessary fields. Do not import payment-card data, identity documents, health information, special requests containing sensitive information, or free-text notes unless strictly required and separately reviewed.
4. Import suppression/unsubscribe status and preserve it.
5. Configure preference center and unsubscribe links for promotional emails.
6. Keep transactional/service communications separate from marketing campaigns.
7. Define a retention period and deletion process for stale contacts.
8. Use role-based access and unique staff accounts.
9. Protect exports in transit and delete temporary working copies after verified import.
10. Record the system owner, hosting location, email provider and backup responsibility in the customer handover.

This checklist is an operational control, not legal advice. The property remains responsible for confirming its lawful basis, privacy notice and marketing compliance in the jurisdictions where it operates and contacts guests.
