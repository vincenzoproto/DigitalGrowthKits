# Repeat Guest Engine — Founder Scope (€690)

## Commercial scope

Founder price: €690 one-time for the first 3 completed purchases.
Standard setup price after founder slots: €990 one-time.
Optional managed service: €129/month.

The founder setup covers one property and one approved guest-data source.

## Included

1. 15-minute setup review before implementation.
2. Review of one CSV/PMS export structure.
3. Mapping of the fields required for segmentation.
4. Separation of promotional-eligible contacts from suppressed/unsubscribed contacts based on the status confirmed by the property.
5. Core segments:
   - recent guests 0–90 days;
   - dormant guests 180+ days;
   - low-season visitors;
   - high-value guests;
   - language/country cohorts where useful;
   - suppressed/unsubscribed.
6. Three configured automations:
   - post-stay relationship;
   - 180-day win-back;
   - low-season fill.
7. Test with seed contacts controlled by the hotel/team.
8. Reporting view.
9. 30-minute staff handover.
10. Basic operating documentation.

## Not included in €690

- PMS custom development or unsupported proprietary integrations;
- custom API development;
- data cleaning beyond reasonable mapping/deduplication;
- copywriting or translation beyond the three launch flows unless agreed;
- ongoing campaign management;
- email/SMS provider fees;
- hosting/VPS or paid third-party services;
- legal advice or determination of the hotel's lawful basis for marketing;
- migration of unrelated CRM or booking systems.

## Qualification before payment

Do not ask the property to pay until these points are confirmed:

- property name and website;
- PMS / booking stack;
- source of historical guest data;
- approximate database size;
- whether a CSV/export is available;
- whether the property can identify marketing-eligible and suppressed/unsubscribed records;
- sender domain / email infrastructure;
- direct-booking destination;
- required languages;
- main commercial objective;
- no technical blocker requiring custom development outside the package.

If the property cannot export the required data or requires unsupported custom integration, quote separately or decline the founder setup.
